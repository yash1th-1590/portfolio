The Story So Far

I'm Yashwanth, a Computer Science undergraduate at Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Technology, Hyderabad, with a sharp focus on backend engineering and applied artificial intelligence. I care less about following tutorials and more about understanding why a system is built the way it is — which is what pulled me toward backend architecture, data flow, and AI-assisted tooling.

Over the past year I've moved from learning fundamentals to actually shipping software: REST APIs that hold up under real use, database schemas that don't fall apart at scale, and AI integrations that solve problems instead of just demoing well. My approach is simple — understand the problem deeply, design before coding, and write systems that other engineers can read and extend.

Academically, I've maintained a 9.18 CGPA while topping the NPTEL national rankings in Advanced Computational Techniques, and I keep stacking certifications in data science, analytics, and systems fundamentals to stay technically well-rounded rather than narrowly specialised.
